Paragraph 1

Leo sat on his back porch in a quiet coastal town,
holding a bright red paper kite with a long tail of colorful ribbon.
The sky above was pale blue,
and the air felt still and heavy against his cheeks.
For weeks, the wind had refused to blow,
leaving the kite trapped inside a dusty cardboard box.
Today, a faint rustle moved through the maple trees at the edge of the yard,
giving Leo a sudden spark of hope.

Paragraph 2

He ran across the damp grass,
lifting the kite high above his head as his sneakers hit the dirt path.
He let out a burst of string and sprinted as fast as his legs could carry him.
For a fleeting second,the kite caught a pocket of air, tilted upward,
and seemed ready to dance among the clouds.
Then, a sudden drop in the breeze hit, and the red paper plunged downward,
landing softly in a patch of wild clover.

Paragraph 3

Leo stopped, staring down at the crumpled toy with a heavy sigh.
His shoulders slumped,
and the excitement in his chest faded into a quiet,
familiar disappointment.
He sat down on a flat stone nearby,
winding the loose string slowly around his fingers.
He remembered how his grandfather used to say
that the wind only hides when it wants you to learn patience.
