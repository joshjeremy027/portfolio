Paragraph 4

With a deep breath,
Leo stood back up and walked to the crest of the small hill behind his house,
where the air moved more freely from the sea.
He tossed the kite upward once more,
waiting for the exact moment the tall grass began to wave.
A fresh gust swept in from the water,
catching the paper frame with a sharp snap and pulling it hard against his palm.
He fed the spool out quickly,
his eyes locking onto the bright red shape as it climbed higher and higher into the open blue.
